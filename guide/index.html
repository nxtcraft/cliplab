How VideoLab Works

VideoLab uses FFmpeg.wasm — the industry-standard FFmpeg library compiled to WebAssembly — running entirely inside your browser tab. This means:

  • Your videos never leave your device. There is no server involved after the initial page load.
  • No watermarks — VideoLab adds nothing to your video output.
  • No file size limits imposed by a server. File size is limited only by your device's RAM.
  • Works offline once FFmpeg.wasm is cached by your browser's Service Worker.

On first load, the browser downloads ~25 MB of WebAssembly. This takes 5–15 seconds and is cached for future visits. Processing time depends on your CPU — video tasks are more CPU-intensive than audio.

Video Trimmer — Step by Step

Trim any video to a specific start and end time — to cut a clip, remove an intro, or extract a scene.

✂️
Video Trimmer
Cut video to any start and end time
  1. Open VideoLab and wait for FFmpeg.wasm to load (progress bar shown on first visit).
  2. Click the Trim tab.
  3. Drag and drop your video, or click Browse Files. Supported: MP4, MOV, AVI, MKV, WebM, FLV.
  4. The video player appears. Play the video to find your desired start point. Click Set Start at the right moment, or type the time manually in mm:ss format.
  5. Find your desired end point and click Set End, or type manually.
  6. Click Trim Video. Processing time depends on the clip length and your CPU speed.
  7. When done, the trimmed video plays in the preview player. Click Download to save.
Fast trim (stream copy): VideoLab uses FFmpeg's stream copy mode for trimming where possible — this means the video is NOT re-encoded, so it trims near-instantly with zero quality loss. Full re-encoding is only done when converting formats or applying effects.

WhatsApp tip: WhatsApp limits video attachments to 16 MB. For a 1-minute 1080p video (~100 MB), use Medium preset to bring it to ~25–35 MB, then trim 20–30 seconds of non-essential content to fit under 16 MB. For longer videos, use the Low preset.

CRF Explained

CRF (Constant Rate Factor) is a quality-based compression setting used by H.264 and H.265 encoders. Lower CRF = higher quality + larger file. Higher CRF = lower quality + smaller file. CRF 18 is visually lossless; CRF 51 is the worst quality.

Slow Motion Effect

Apply a slow motion effect to any video clip. VideoLab uses FFmpeg's setpts and atempo filters to slow down both video and audio.

Speed FactorEffectExample Use

Frame rate note: True slow motion requires high frame rate source video (60fps or 120fps). Slowing down a 30fps video creates motion blur and stuttering. For the smoothest slow motion, use video shot at 60fps or higher as your input.

Add Audio to Video

Replace or add an audio track to any video. Use cases include:

  • Add background music to a silent video
  • Replace poor audio with a cleaned-up recording
  • Add a voiceover to a screen recording
  • Create a lyric video by combining an image video with an audio track
  1. Click the Add Audio tab.
  2. Upload your video file.
  3. Upload your audio file (MP3, WAV, AAC, OGG).
  4. Choose whether to Replace the existing audio or Mix with it.
  5. Click Apply and download the result.

Extract Audio (Video to MP3)

Extract the audio track from any video file as an MP3 or AAC file.

  1. Click the Extract Audio tab (or the Mute tab — both show audio extraction).
  2. Upload your video file.
  3. Select output format: MP3 for maximum compatibility, AAC for better quality at the same size.
  4. Click Extract Audio. The audio is separated without re-encoding when possible.
  5. Download the audio file.
Lossless extraction: When a video's audio track is already in MP3 or AAC format (most MP4 files), VideoLab extracts it without re-encoding — meaning zero quality loss. Re-encoding only happens when you request a format different from the source track.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is VideoLab really free?
Yes, VideoLab is completely free — no sign-up, no watermarks, no file limits. All processing runs in your browser using FFmpeg.wasm. Your videos never leave your device.
How much can VideoLab compress a video?
Compression results depend on the original video's codec and content. Typical results: a 100 MB MP4 from a smartphone compresses to 20–40 MB at Medium quality with no visible degradation. Screen recordings with static content often compress by 80–90%.
Can I compress a video for WhatsApp?
Yes. WhatsApp has a 16 MB limit for video attachments. Use VideoLab's compressor with the Medium or Low quality preset to bring your video under 16 MB. For longer videos, also trim unwanted sections first using the Trim tab.
Why is video processing slow?
Video encoding is CPU-intensive. Processing speed depends on your device's CPU speed and the video's resolution and length. A 1-minute 1080p video may take 2–5 minutes to compress on a mid-range laptop. Trimming (stream copy) is near-instant because it skips encoding.
Can I extract audio (MP3) from a video?
Yes. Use the Extract Audio tab. Upload any video file, select MP3 or AAC as the output format, and click Extract. VideoLab strips the audio track without re-encoding when possible, so quality is preserved from the original.
Does VideoLab add a watermark?
No. VideoLab adds no watermarks, logos, or any modifications to your video output. The output is a clean, unmodified version of your input (minus the edits you chose). This is because all processing happens on your device — there's no server to inject anything.
